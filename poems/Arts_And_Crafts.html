Arts and Crafts

All the children line the table like neat utensils on my aunt’s cloth napkins    
When dinner is called they immediately rumple and contort into the 
Ghost graves below where 
My cousin’s body lies

Picking up the pieces is easy 
But first you have to cut them out

I give squares to the young ones 
All the colors of the rainbow
And watch their warm gooey hands 
Paste them on thinning looseleaf

For the adults shadows shape them better
They come after the sun
Folding silhouette slivers 
Smelling of flat dreams and compressed cardboard

I sip scissors when my paper mache dolls cry
It tickles my throat
Following step by step instructions 
Back down the alley way where 
Cigarette smoke floats to the top of canvasses
And roses spout from the chests 
Of simple scars
