Blue Moon Body

Under the midnight sun
I lay swaddled by the sea
Soon to be soothed to sleep by
Siren serenades 

Along the currents that shape my body are
Soft scars that search for you
Rippling through every wave
Reaching every shore, lapping onto
Silver stones stained by Selene’s tears

At last I can pour out from myself
Encumber the water in my own basin
Let salt lick my wounds which
Warm them like embers under moonlight and
Yearn for their pain as I do for you

Beneath me there is nothing now
But the subtle rhythm of the ocean
Turning over itself
Twisting away

Wash over me
Over and over
Seep into me
Sleek, silent and secret
Until I am swept away
