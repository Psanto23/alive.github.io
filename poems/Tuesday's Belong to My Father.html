Tuesday's Belong to My Father

Tuesday afternoons were deep orange hues
Mixed with sauteed onions and
Miles and Johnny jamming from the ridges of the record player

Clutter decorated the living room
Boughs of newspapers on couch cushions
CD cases adorned the dining room table
Where my mother saw ruins I saw a pantheon to my father

There he stood, at the center of it all
His hair well seasoned much like his meals
His face rosy cooled by complex blue eyes
His kisses scratchy and itchy when he picked me up
Patience and peace rose off him like the steam from the saucepans

It’s pretty pathetic 
How quickly our minds adapt
And soon I took the magic for granted
Mixing orange hues with colder grays of an angsty teen

But now that I’m away
And have my own colors 
I’ll never forget
How Tuesday’s belong to my father
